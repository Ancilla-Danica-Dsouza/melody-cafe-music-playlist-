# Themes

Click a coloured dot at the top right of the app to switch.

| Group | Themes |
|---|---|
| Classic | Cafe Brown, Midnight Blue, Ocean Breeze |
| Soft and pretty | Blush Pink, Lavender Dream, Peach Sunset, Rose Gold, Cotton Candy |
| UAE | UAE - Dubai Gold, UAE - Desert Dunes, UAE - Burj Nights, UAE - Flag Colours, UAE - Arabian Nights, UAE - Oasis |

## Add your own
Open `melody_cafe/ui/themes.py`, copy an entry, rename it and change the colours.
Required keys: `bg panel text muted accent button btn_text sel sel_text log deco tagline stripe`.
`python -m unittest` checks every theme has them.
